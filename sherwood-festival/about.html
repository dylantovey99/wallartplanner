About Sherwood Festival

Building Community Through Celebration

Our Story

The Sherwood Community Festival has been bringing our local community together for years, creating a vibrant celebration of culture, creativity, and connection in the beautiful Sherwood Arboretum.

What started as a small gathering has grown into one of Brisbane's most beloved community events, attracting thousands of visitors each year while maintaining its grassroots, community-focused spirit.

🎪
🤝

100% Volunteer-Run

We're incredibly proud to be a completely volunteer-run organization. Every single person involved in the Sherwood Community Festival gives their time, energy, and passion because they believe in the power of community.

There are no paid staff - just dedicated community members who work tirelessly throughout the year to plan, organize, and deliver this amazing event.

Not-For-Profit Community Organization

The Sherwood Community Festival is a not-for-profit, member-based Incorporated Association. All funds raised go directly back into making the festival bigger and better each year, supporting local artists and community groups, and strengthening our community bonds.

Our Values

What Drives Our Community

🌟

Community First

Everything we do is for the community, by the community. We prioritize inclusivity, accessibility, and creating spaces where everyone feels welcome.

🎨

Celebrate Local

We showcase local talent, support local businesses, and highlight the incredible creativity and diversity within our community.

💚

Sustainability

We're committed to minimizing our environmental impact and promoting sustainable practices throughout the festival.

🙌

Volunteer Spirit

We believe in the power of people coming together to create something special. Our volunteers are the heart of everything we do.

Join Our Team

Hundreds of Hours, Countless Memories

Our volunteer committee works year-round to bring the festival to life. From planning and logistics to day-of coordination, every aspect of the festival is managed by passionate community members.

In 2024, despite torrential rain forcing the cancellation of the Saturday event, our volunteers showed incredible resilience and dedication. Hundreds of hours of preparation demonstrated the unwavering commitment of our community.
