Frequently Asked Questions

Quick Answers to Common Questions

When is the next Sherwood Community Festival?

The festival is typically held annually in November. The 2025 festival date will be announced soon. Follow us on Facebook for updates!

Is the festival free to attend?

Yes! The Sherwood Community Festival is completely free to attend. We're a not-for-profit community event focused on bringing people together.

How can I volunteer?

We'd love to have you! Use the contact form above and select "Volunteer Inquiry" as the subject. We'll get back to you with information about available volunteer opportunities.

Can I bring my dog?

While we love pets, please check with the Sherwood Arboretum's pet policy before bringing animals. Service animals are always welcome.

Are there food and drinks available?

Absolutely! We have a variety of food vendors and stalls offering diverse cuisines, beverages, and treats throughout the festival.

Is the venue accessible?

Yes, the Sherwood Arboretum has accessible pathways and facilities. If you have specific accessibility requirements, please contact us in advance.
