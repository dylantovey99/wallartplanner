Our Stages

Multiple Venues Showcasing Local Talent

🎤 Arbor Green Stage

Main Stage - Premier Performances

Our main stage features the festival's headlining acts and most popular performances. With professional sound and lighting, the Arbor Green Stage provides a concert-quality experience in the heart of the Arboretum.

🌳 Fig Tree Grove Stage

Acoustic & Intimate Performances

Nestled under the beautiful fig trees, this stage offers a more intimate setting for acoustic performances, spoken word, and community presentations. It's the perfect spot to relax and enjoy the natural beauty of the Arboretum.

Street Performances & Emerging Artists

A free-form space where street performers, emerging artists, and spontaneous entertainment comes alive. Buskers' Corner celebrates the raw energy and creativity of street performance culture.
