Welcome to Sherwood Festival

A Not-For-Profit Community Celebration

🌳

Community Spirit

The Sherwood Community Festival is a not-for-profit, member-based Incorporated Association run entirely by dedicated volunteers from our local community.

🎭

Local Talent

Showcasing the best of Brisbane's local artists, performers, and community groups across multiple stages and venues throughout the Arboretum.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family Fun

A full day of activities, workshops, entertainment, and markets designed to bring families and neighbors together in celebration.

Volunteer Opportunities

Help Make the Magic Happen

Everyone Involved is a Volunteer

The Sherwood Community Festival is made possible entirely through the dedication of volunteers. There are no paid staff - just passionate community members who want to create something special.

We're always looking for enthusiastic volunteers to help with:

  • Event setup and pack down
  • Stage management
  • Arts Hub activities
  • Stallholder coordination
  • General festival support
