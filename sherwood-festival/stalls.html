Stallholder Information

What You Need to Know

Requirements & Guidelines

  • All stalls must be setup and ready before 2:00 PM (festival opening time)
  • Stalls must remain operational for the entire festival duration (2 PM - 8 PM)
  • Food vendors must have current food safety certifications and licenses
  • Public liability insurance is required for all stallholders
  • Stallholders must provide their own tables, chairs, and display equipment
  • All products must comply with Australian consumer law and safety standards
  • Stallholders are responsible for their own waste management and site cleanup

Power & Water: Limited power points are available on a first-come, first-served basis. Please indicate your requirements when applying. Water access is available in designated areas.

Application Process

  • Complete the stallholder application form (available closer to the event)
  • Provide required documentation (insurance, licenses if applicable)
  • Submit payment with your application
  • Receive confirmation and site allocation details
  • Attend the pre-festival briefing (optional but recommended)

Important: Stall spaces are limited and allocated on a first-come, first-served basis. Early application is strongly recommended. Applications typically open 3-4 months before the festival date.

What's Included

  • Designated stall space (size varies by stall type)
  • Inclusion in festival marketing materials
  • Access to facilities and amenities
  • Support from festival volunteers and coordinators
  • Opportunity to showcase to thousands of visitors

Community Group Discounts: Registered not-for-profit community groups and charities may be eligible for reduced stall fees. Please inquire when applying.
